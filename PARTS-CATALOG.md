# IndieDuck printed parts catalog

Every printed part of IndieDuck Cyber Edition on platform P1, with its code and current revision. Codes starting with **P** are platform parts (shared by every edition); **C** are Cyber Edition parts. Use the codes to label parts bags or to tell parts apart; the revision tells you whether a print is current (see [what changed and what to reprint](PARTS-CHANGELOG.md)).

Parts marked "prints with" are printed as one piece with that part, in a second colour: print that part from its **multi-colour** file. This page is generated by `tools/part_versions.py` from `COMPONENTS.csv` and `PART-VERSIONS.json`.

55 printed parts: 27 platform, 28 Cyber Edition.
