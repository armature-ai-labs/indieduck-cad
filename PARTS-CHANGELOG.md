# What changed in the printed parts

Every printed IndieDuck part has a code and a revision (r1, r2, ...); the [parts catalog](PARTS-CATALOG.md) lists them all. A part's revision goes up whenever its shape changes; a print of an older revision may not fit the newer parts around it. This page is generated by `tools/part_versions.py` from `PART-VERSIONS.json`; the revision of each part is also in the `part_rev` column of `COMPONENTS.csv`.

## What to reprint

Find the release you printed from (the tag, or the date you downloaded or sliced the files). Reprint the parts listed for it.
